## Phase State Machine

The market schedule decomposes any Unix timestamp into one of four operational phases:

| Phase | Time Window | Max Leverage | Mode | Funding Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`OPEN`** | Mon–Fri 14:30 – 20:00 UTC | **20.00x** | Normal Trading | 1.00x Standard |
| **`PRE_CLOSE`** | Mon–Fri 20:00 – 21:00 UTC | **Linear 20x $\to$ 5x** | Deleveraging Warning | 1.00x Standard |
| **`CLOSED`** | Fri 21:00 – Mon 14:30 UTC | **5.00x** | Reduce-Only | 2.00x Defensive |
| **`REOPEN_COOLDOWN`** | Mon 14:30 – 14:45 UTC | **Linear 5x $\to$ 20x** | Liquidations Active | 1.00x Standard |

## 1. Linear Deleveraging Ramp (`PRE_CLOSE`)

During the 3,600 seconds leading up to Friday 21:00 UTC, the maximum allowable leverage $L_{\max}(t)$ tightens linearly:

$$L_{\max}(t) = L_{\text{closed}} + (L_{\text{open}} - L_{\text{closed}}) \times \frac{t_{\text{close}} - t}{\Delta t_{\text{pre\_close}}}$$

Where:
* $L_{\text{open}} = 20.00\text{x}$
* $L_{\text{closed}} = 5.00\text{x}$
* $\Delta t_{\text{pre\_close}} = 3,600\text{ s}$

## 2. Automated Keeper Deleveraging

In the final 10 minutes before market close ($T - 10\text{m}$ to $T$), keeper bots can invoke `deleverage()` on any position exceeding the upcoming 5.00x weekend limit:

```solidity
function deleverage(address trader) external onlyRole(KEEPER_ROLE);
```

### Deleveraging Mechanics:
1. **Executed at Fair Market Prices:** Positions are trimmed at the active Friday close price ($4,150.00) rather than at a panicked, discounted Monday open.
2. **Trader Margin Preserved:** Traders receive the excess margin back into their account balance; they are not liquidated.
3. **Vault Protected:** Because high-leverage risk is retired before market close, the liquidity vault carries zero 20x exposure across the weekend scoreboard blackout.
